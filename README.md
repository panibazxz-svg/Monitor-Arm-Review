Monitor Arm Review: Desk Clamp depth and Footprint tested
Your monitor stand is eating six inches of desk space you don’t have, and your keyboard is already pushed against the edge. This review tests four popular monitor arms on a 24-inch deep desk to find out which ones actually clear the back edge without hitting a wall, a shelf, or your gaming chair. Mount is the one that came out ahead on our depth and stability tests, and we’ll show you exactly why below.

What we Tested and Why Monitor Arm Clamp Depth Matters More Than Weight Capacity
Most monitor arm reviews focus on weight capacity and cable management, which matters. But on a shallow desk, the real constraint : how far back the mounting hardware sits, and how far forward the arm’s folded resting position pushes when you’re not using it. A 32-inch monitor on a 42-inch-wide, 24-inch-deep desk leaves almost no margin for error. We measured four arms end-to-end: clamp footprint, folded depth, extended reach, and how much desk space each one gave back compared to the stock stand it replaced.
